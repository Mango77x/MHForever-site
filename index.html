A native program, not an emulator

The game's code is translated ahead of time into native PC code, and the console's system services are re-implemented. The result is a normal Windows program that runs the game directly, with a modern launcher around it.

Native, not emulated

No emulator runs in the middle. That is what makes higher resolutions, any controller and, later, online play practical.

Your copy, your files

MHForever never includes or downloads game assets. Everything is read from the disc image you provide, and your saves stay on your computer.

Built to grow

Each game is a module on a shared runtime. Freedom Unite is the first one. More games and regions are planned.

European release, ULES01213

Play

Smoother than the original

The original game runs at 30 frames per second. Since version 0.2.0 MHForever can draw extra frames in between, up to 180 fps, so motion looks smoother on a fast screen. Compare the options.

Experimental Choose it in the launcher Settings or in the in-game menu (press Esc). Busy scenes may not hold the highest rates. On a 60 Hz screen, anything above 60 looks the same as 60.

Playable game
Monster Hunter Freedom Unite (PSP), European release, known in Japan as Monster Hunter Portable 2nd G. It is the only game for now.
Frame rate
30 fps like the original console, or 60 to 180 fps with frame interpolation (experimental). You can change it while playing.
Online play
Not available yet. Internet multiplayer is being designed.
Platforms
Windows and Linux, 64-bit. Steam Deck is untested.
Controllers
Gamepads work out of the box. Tested with a PS5 DualSense. Other pads recognized by SDL should work.
Resolution
Up to 10 times the original 480×272, with an optional smoother texture filter. You can change it while playing.
Other games and regions
The US and Japanese releases of Freedom Unite and other games of the series are planned.
In-game menu
Press Escape (or the controller's guide button) to pause: change the volume, resolution, frame rate and fullscreen without restarting. It works with the keyboard, a controller or the mouse.
Saves
The same format as the PSP and PPSSPP: import your own saves from a PSP or PPSSPP, and copy MHForever's saves into PPSSPP. Your own version of the game only.

Questions

Short answers to what people ask first.

Do I need to own the game?

Yes. MHForever requires your own legitimate copy of Freedom Unite. No game assets are included or downloaded.

Which version works?

The European release of Monster Hunter Freedom Unite for PSP (ULES01213, v1.01). The US and Japanese releases are planned.

Is this an emulator?

No. The game's program is recompiled into native PC code and the console's system services are re-implemented. You get a normal Windows program.

Is it legal?

MHForever contains no Capcom or Sony code or assets and asks you to use your own copy. It is not affiliated with Capcom or Sony. This is not legal advice, and laws differ by country.

Can I play online with friends?

Not yet. Internet multiplayer is being designed so you can hunt with friends without a PSP.

Does it work on Linux or Steam Deck?

Linux, yes: since 0.4.1 there is a Linux download next to the Windows one (it needs WebKitGTK 4.1 and GTK 3, and is built on Ubuntu 24.04, so very old distributions may not run it). Steam Deck has not been tested yet; reports are welcome.

How do I report a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub with your version and what happened, or ask in the Discord. Reports are welcome.

Will I lose my saves when I update?

No. Saves live in Documents\MHForever\saves and your settings in %APPDATA%\MHForever, outside the program folder, so unzipping a new version never touches them. Saves from older versions that sat next to the program are copied over the first time you press Play.

Can I use saves from a real PSP or PPSSPP?

Yes, from your own version of the game (the European release for now). In the launcher Settings, under Saves, choose the folder that holds them. Your current save is kept in a backup folder. MHForever's saves also load in PPSSPP. A real PSP is not guaranteed to accept them.

Which frame rates are available?

The original runs at 30 fps. In the launcher Settings you can pick 60 to 180 fps (any multiple of 30): the game still runs at 30 and extra pictures are drawn in between. It is experimental, and heavy scenes may not hold the highest rates.

Is there a community?

Yes, a Discord server for help, bug reports, suggestions and release news. Bring your own copy of the game: there is no piracy talk there.

Join the Discord

Can I change graphics or sound while playing?

Yes. Press Escape in the game and a menu opens where you can change the volume, the resolution (up to 10x), the frame rate (30 to 180) and fullscreen without restarting.
